Engineered for Raw Speed & Reliability

Modern architecture designed to maximize your bandwidth with zero stutter.

🚀

Multi-Stream Acceleration

Splits files dynamically into parallel chunks (up to 32 socket streams), saturating high-speed fiber & gigabit connections.

🪟

Native Windows Shell

Full support for Windows 11 and 10 with native WPF UI, system tray integration, automatic startup, and clipboard monitoring.

🐧

Native Wayland & CSD

Runs natively on Linux without XWayland. Supports KDE Plasma 6, GNOME 46+, Sway, and Hyprland with StatusNotifierItem tray.

🎬

Streaming Video Grabber

Integrated yt-dlp engine automatically captures video and audio streams from YouTube, Vimeo, and 1000+ streaming sites.

🧩

Browser Extensions (MV3)

One-click download takeover and media capture for Chrome, Brave, Edge, Firefox, and Floorp via fast local loopback IPC.

📱

Android & MTP-Safe Names

Automatically sanitizes illegal characters in downloaded file names, preventing copy errors over USB/MTP transfers to phones and tablets.
